OUR STORY

Big dreams start small.

Huon Prints is a small 3D-printing business started by two teenage girls in Tasmania's Huon Valley. We're learning about creativity, customer service, running a business and managing money while saving towards our future education.

Thanks for supporting our journey, one print at a time!

OUR MATERIALS

Thoughtful 3D printing

Many of our prints use PLA, a plant-derived plastic commonly made using starches from crops such as corn. PLA generally requires specialised industrial composting conditions to biodegrade; it does not readily break down in home compost or landfill. Lamps and other products may also contain electronic or non-PLA components.
